# Humanloop ph7 — portfolio production deliverables

This is the working production list for turning the current Humanloop concept into a portfolio case study that reads at an experienced industrial-design studio level. Do not present a planned activity as completed evidence.

## 1. Narrative and evidence

| Priority | Deliverable | Current input | Definition of done |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | One-sentence project thesis | Repository brief | A crisp tension, intervention, and value proposition that can be understood without AI jargon. |
| P0 | Role, scope, duration, collaborators | Not yet documented | Names personal ownership, team boundaries, timeline, constraints, and what was inherited. |
| P0 | Research synthesis | Research folders are largely empty | 6–10 relevant interviews or contextual inquiries, direct observations, current workflow map, affinity synthesis, and 3–5 prioritized insights. |
| P0 | Competitive landscape | Questions exist; study is not complete | Fair comparison of software approval tools, ambient e-paper devices, pagers/notification hardware, and single-purpose desk objects. |
| P0 | Design principles | Strong source material exists | Three memorable principles tied directly to observed user needs and later design decisions. |
| P0 | Outcome story | Requires functional prototype | Baseline versus one-week trial: interruption response time, completion rate, false urgency, trust, and qualitative behavior change. |

## 2. Industrial design process

| Priority | Deliverable | Required views / evidence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Form exploration | 30–50 thumbnail sketches, 6–10 coherent directions, and a down-select rationale—not a single polished concept. |
| P0 | Ergonomic studies | One-handed reach overlays, left/right-hand use, desk-angle studies, button and dial force targets, grip/pickup analysis, and scale photos. |

| P0 | Appearance model | Full-scale foam/resin looks-like model in the chosen CMF, photographed beside real desk objects for scale. |

| P0 | CMF specification | 6061 aluminum, blast grade, Type II natural-anodize thickness, laser-etch contrast/depth, display-lens finish, gasket, polymer RF-window resin/color, and approved physical samples. |
| P0 | DFM package | Native parametric CAD, STEP, STL, tolerance stack, section views, fastener strategy, service access, and dimensioned drawings with GD&T where it matters. |
| P1 | Design language system | Radius/chamfer logic, gap and flush strategy, control proportions, graphic placement, typography, and how the language extends to future Humanloop clients. |
| P1 | Sustainability and repair | Disassembly sequence, battery replacement, screw and adhesive rationale, material separation, repair access, and lifecycle assumptions. |

## 3. Validation deliverables

| Priority | Deliverable | Method and output |
| --- | --- | --- |
| P0 | Glanceability test | Test at 0.5 m, 1 m, and 1.5 m, multiple light levels and view angles; publish task accuracy and scan time. |
| P0 | Interaction usability test | 5–8 representative users; approval, rejection, queue navigation, voice input, offline recovery; publish success, time, and observed errors. |
| P0 | Control feel study | Measure encoder torque/detents, button force/travel, hold threshold, haptic amplitude, sound, and subjective preference. |
| P0 | RF window experiment | Bare baseline, sealed foil body, then window at 3/5/8 mm clearance; record RSSI, one-hour loss, reconnect, and interrupt latency. |
| P0 | Power characterization | Instrument the whole device at the cell/regulator; publish connected idle, refresh, voice, haptic, and honest battery runtime. |
| P0 | Thermal and desk stability | Surface temperature, center of gravity, push/turn stability, cable insertion, and rubber-foot behavior. |
| P1 | Finish and durability | Abrasion, fingerprints/cleaning, edge damage, etch legibility, anodize color match, and button-bore behavior after coating. |

## 4. Photography and rendered stills

Every final image needs a job in the story. A studio-level case study should have:

1. One unmistakable cover keyshot.
2. Front, side, rear, and top orthographic views.
3. In-context desk shot with hand and nearby objects for scale.
4. Dial knurl, button travel, display lens, USB-C, back etch, RF window, chamfer, and fastener macro details.
5. Early form-study contact sheet and down-select wall.
6. Resin revision lineup from A through D, annotated with what each taught.
7. UI state sheet rendered on the real e-paper panel, not only a flat mock.
8. RF and power test setup photographs with measured results.
9. Final machined prototype beauty set and one-week-in-use record.

### Exact rendering style and lighting environment

- Visual language: photoreal physically based industrial-design CGI, quiet editorial product photography, physically plausible tolerances and contact shadows. No concept-art fog, blue sci-fi accents, floating parts, glossy chrome, or nonphysical display glow.
- Color pipeline: ACEScg, 16-bit EXR master, restrained warm-neutral grade, 6% fine grain in finishing.
- Hero camera: 70 mm full-frame equivalent, camera around device mid-height, three-quarter view, f/8 aesthetic, minimal perspective distortion.
- Macro camera: 100 mm, shallow but readable depth of field, focus pulls only when they explain a detail.
- Set: continuous warm bone mineral-plaster sweep with a matte off-white work surface; no visual clutter.
- Key: 150 cm octagonal soft source, high camera-left at roughly 45°, 4800 K.
- Edge: narrow 50 × 180 cm strip camera-right, 5600 K, controlled to reveal the chamfer and dial knurl without clipping.
- Fill: broad white card low and near camera; target a 4:1 key-to-fill ratio.
- Accent: optional weak 3200 K rear practical, never visible as a colored streak on the product.
- Shadow: soft 12 cm contact shadow, enough density to establish mass and stability.
- Material calibration: micro-bead-blasted 6061 with clear Type II anodize; tune roughness and normal scale against a photographed shop sample, not the concept render.

## 5. Motion deliverables

The copy-ready video-generation prompts are kept outside the public website in [`HUMANLOOP_VEO3_PROMPTS.md`](HUMANLOOP_VEO3_PROMPTS.md). Produce a 16:9 4K master, captioned web export, clean sound-design stems, a poster frame, and an 8-second loop for each final sequence.

## 6. Page and presentation craft

- Keep the story to 8–12 decisive beats; move raw documentation to appendices.
- Use process artifacts at a readable size. Avoid mood-board collages with no captions.
- Caption each artifact with what changed because of it.
- Label concept render, prototype, test, and production intent accurately.
- Show two or three rejected directions and why they lost.
- Put the strongest evidence in the first 30 seconds: cover, thesis, role, scope, key interaction, and one proof point.
- Make the page keyboard accessible, responsive, fast, and respectful of reduced-motion settings.
- Export a concise PDF version for interviews: 16–24 pages, same thesis and evidence hierarchy, with no website-only interactions required to understand it.

## Recommended production order

1. Role/scope/timeline and research plan.
2. Workflow research and competitive audit.
3. Form/control exploration and full-scale looks-like models.
4. UI and interaction validation with mocks.
5. Bench electronics and measured component library.
6. Integrated resin revisions plus RF and power testing.
7. Final CAD, CMF samples, drawings, tolerance review, and DFM.
8. Machined prototype and one-week outcome study.
9. Final photography, diagrams, and motion pieces.
