# Humanloop ph7 — Veo 3 video prompts

These prompts are production text only. They are intentionally not part of the public portfolio website. Use the current Humanloop ph7 product image as the visual reference for every generation.

## Shared visual language

- Format: 16:9, 4K, 24 fps, 180-degree shutter, photoreal physically based industrial-design film.
- Product: preserve the exact ph7 proportions, natural-anodized 6061 aluminum body, dark recessed portrait e-paper surround, circular mic button, SELECT and BACK controls, and right-side knurled dial.
- Screen system: one-bit black and white, no gray and no glow. Large bold geometric sans-serif `HUMANLOOP`, one short black dash, monospaced supporting text, extreme whitespace, no boxes, charts, progress bars, icons, or fictional HUD overlays.
- Studio: continuous warm bone mineral-plaster sweep with a matte off-white desk surface.
- Key light: 150 cm octagonal softbox high camera-left at 45 degrees, 4800 K.
- Edge light: 50 × 180 cm strip camera-right, 5600 K, controlled to reveal the chamfer and dial knurl without clipping.
- Fill: broad white card low and near camera, approximately 4:1 key-to-fill ratio.
- Product finish: micro-bead-blasted aluminum, clear Type II anodize, crisp edge breaks, subtle realistic contact shadows, no fingerprints.
- Avoid: blue or colored light, fog, glossy chrome, floating hardware, screen glow, lens flares, futuristic UI, unnecessary camera moves, extra ports or controls, distorted typography, logos other than Humanloop, and watermarks.
