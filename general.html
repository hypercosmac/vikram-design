HOW IT WORKS

The General Co. behaves like a generalist operations hire: it listens in Slack and your messaging apps, understands intent, and either executes with AI or escalates to the best human on our crew.

Text-first interface
Work + life ops
AI + human-in-the-loop
INPUT

1. Text it like your ops lead

You speak in plain language. The General Co. handles:

  • “Plan our offsite and book travel for the team.”
  • “Renew my car insurance and send me the documents.”
  • “Schedule a follow-up with the vendor and chase the revised contract.”
  • “Move my car for street cleaning — find someone to do it if I’m not around.”
CONTROL

4. Manual escalation, on your terms

At any moment, you can push a task to human ops with a single message:

  • “Escalate this to a human.”
  • “Have someone call support and resolve this today.”

Manual escalations are always available at your discretion (rate limits apply by plan) — giving you a safety lever whenever details matter.

ROUTING

2. AI decides what needs to happen

Our routing engine evaluates each task and determines:

  • Can we fully automate this?
  • Do we need a human operator?
  • Is this personal, team-wide, or both?
  • Which tools, vendors, or accounts are involved?

Tasks are broken into steps, prioritized, and assigned a resolution path — with clear status inside Slack or your messaging thread.

EXECUTION

3. The right operator, every time

When a human is needed, AI chooses the most apt operator based on:

  • Skillset (travel, legal admin, finance ops, home services, etc.)
  • Task type and required systems
  • Time zone and language
  • Past performance on similar tasks

You don’t have to manage “who does what.” The General Co. orchestrates the right crew for each job.

MANUAL OVERRIDE

You’re never locked out of control

At any point, you can override the routing and force a human escalation:

  • “Escalate this to a human now.”
  • “Have someone call this vendor and sort it out.”
  • “Route this to finance ops only.”

Manual escalation is always available, with rate limits by plan to keep the system sustainable and high-signal for everyone.

MATCHING ENGINE

AI chooses the right operator

When the task requires a human, The General Co. evaluates who on our crew is best suited based on:

  • Required tools (e.g., airline portals, government sites, CRMs)
  • Domain knowledge (travel, compliance, vendor management, home services)
  • Regional context (time zone, language, local norms)
  • Performance history and SLA track record

This isn’t a generic ticket queue. It’s a purpose-built routing layer that treats each request like an operations decision.

LEARNING LOOP

Over time, your Personal COO feels less like a service and more like an extension of how you think and operate.
